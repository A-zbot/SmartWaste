About the project

SmartWaste 2.0 — AI-Driven Urban Waste Management

A predictive decision-support system that turns citizen waste complaints into classified, geolocated, clustered and forecasted intelligence for municipal teams.

Problem

Complaints pile up without structure

Municipal call centres receive thousands of waste reports per month. Text is unstructured, duplicate reports are common, hotspots are discovered late, and resource allocation is reactive. Citizens get no visibility of what happens next.

Solution

A closed-loop decision-support pipeline

Every complaint is classified by a trained model, screened for duplicates with NLP similarity, mapped, clustered with DBSCAN, and folded into a demand forecast with honest baselines - all explainable to the operator.

Modules

What the system actually does

BSupervised AI classification

TF-IDF + Logistic Regression / Random Forest, stratified split, 5-fold cross-validation, published metrics and confusion matrices.

CNLP duplicate detection

Cosine similarity over TF-IDF vectors combined with haversine distance and category match - suggestions, never silent rejections.

DDBSCAN hotspots

Haversine DBSCAN over stored coordinates with configurable radius and min-samples; recurring zones surface on the map.

EForecasting

Random Forest regressor on lag/calendar features versus seasonal-naive and moving-average baselines, evaluated on a chronological hold-out.

FExplainability

Signed feature contributions for every prediction plus database-derived context factors (age, duplicates, hotspot).

GDecision support

Backlog, SLA and low-confidence queues translated into concrete, evidence-backed recommendations for operators.

Principles

Honest by design

1No fabricated numbers

Every KPI, chart, recommendation and metric is computed from the live database or a measured evaluation report.

2No fake AI

If a model is missing or uncertain the UI says so; low confidence is flagged for human review instead of being hidden.

3Safe evolution

Schema migrations are forward-only and idempotent so existing complaints survive upgrades.
