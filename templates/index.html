Why SmartWaste 2.0

Beyond a complaint form

AISupervised complaint intelligence

Logistic Regression and Random Forest are compared with cross-validation, and the selected model's real metrics are published on the admin console — accuracy, macro-F1, per-class support and the confusion matrix. Nothing is claimed that has not been measured.

GEOGeospatial hotspot detection

DBSCAN over stored coordinates finds recurring problem zones with a configurable radius and minimum cluster size. Complaints without coordinates are reported honestly rather than silently absorbed into a cluster.

FXPredictive decision support

Daily complaint volumes are forecast with a seasonal baseline versus a Random Forest regressor, evaluated with MAE and RMSE on a chronological hold-out — so the operations team knows exactly how much to trust the number.
